# Explore Drawing references

The camera treatment and drafting-board / assembly / interior interaction were
studied from Samnang Aing's `flat-to-3d` project:
https://github.com/Sam1983Aing/flat-to-3d

This prototype implements project-specific rendering and navigation in TypeScript;
it does not embed his fictional houses, marketing text, brand or unlicensed Lintel
repository. The long-lens/dolly-zoom technique follows the published description
and inspected camera implementation. Attribution and the reference MIT license
are retained below to cover that adaptation.
