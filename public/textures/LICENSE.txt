No attribution is required; it is given here anyway.

  aerial_grass_rock     https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
  brown_mud_leaves_01   https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_leaves_01
  concrete_floor_02     https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02
  gravel_ground_01      https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_ground_01
  wood_table_001        https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001

Each set is its colour (diff), OpenGL normal (nor_gl) and roughness (rough) map,
reduced from the 2k originals to 1024 x 1024 JPEG. wood_table_001_grain_1k.jpg is
derived from wood_table_001's colour map: greyscale, lightened, so a finish colour
can tint the grain.
