Approved 17e2a49 (model 53). Stacked tag + single F below + unique schedule-size pad + unique 500x500 pier strictly inside is tight, and the eccentric-pier test is good.
Source search (all STRUCT, Dunnville), for your applicability judgement:
1. Pier top: S1-1 p2 (1644,1588), under the COLUMN SCHEDULE: "ALL COLUMN BASE PLATES TO BE 300mm BELOW SOG." It applies to every column base plate, so pier top = U/S base plate = SOG − 300 (less the plate/grout). The SOG level is on S1-2: "SLAB BEARING ON 300mm THK. 20mm DIA. CLEAR…" (983,551 / 1998,825) plus the GROUND FLOOR 0 datum you already read. This is the strongest general statement and it ties P-2 top to the slab.
2. Footing bearing depth: S1-0 p1 (457,513) "ALL EXTERIOR FOUNDATIONS SHALL EXTEND A MINIMUM OF 1220mm BELOW FINISHED GRADE FOR FROST PROTECTION. STEP FOOTINGS, WHERE REQUIRED…". This is exterior only and a minimum, so it is NOT a T/O for interior pads.
3. S1-1 p2 (369,1447) "ALL FOOTINGS MUST BEAR ON ENGINEERED FILL (100 kPa SLS, AT LEAST 1220mm BELOW [finished grade]…)". It says ALL FOOTINGS, but again it's a minimum bearing depth, not a top-of-footing elevation. S1-0 (429,600 / 456,644) adds native soil 1300-2300 below grade.
So: P-2 top is source-applicable via note 1. For the interior F-2 top/underside there's only "at least 1220 below finished grade" (note 3). That's a minimum, so I'd keep F-2 extrusion withheld, or show it as "minimum per general note", not a datum.
