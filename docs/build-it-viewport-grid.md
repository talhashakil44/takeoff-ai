# Grid registration for side-by-side services plans (T20)

Build It now registers the **two viewports independently** on a sheet such as Kelso M1-02, M1-03 or M1-04. Each side must retain its level title and pass a matching letter-grid and number-grid fit against the architectural plan. The fit uses labelled bubble positions at the sheet's calibrated scale, accepts at least three labels on each axis within 0.15 m, and rejects a viewport with incomplete or inconsistent evidence. The other viewport can still be placed. Takeoffs on rejected sides retain their quantity and level without shape, as in T15.

The read-only refreshed Kelso audit found 815 registered measurements across the three sheets: 444 at L1 and 371 at L2. All 815 now have 3D run geometry and their matching IFC segments. The M1-02, M1-03 and M1-04 splits remain 214/161, 48/71 and 182/139. The independent offsets of M1-02's two viewports differ by 26.59 m horizontally, which is why a whole-sheet transform would be wrong. An independent placement check found no service run centre outside its level's architectural slab bounding box plus 5 m. This is a placement check, not proof of installed pipe/duct elevation or size.

The model still labels service cross-section and elevation as assumed. It does not derive a pipe diameter, duct section, hanger position or clash-free route from the line alone. `MODEL_VERSION` 19 invalidates cached models from before the viewport XY fix and follows T21's version 18. The current read-only audit reports 1,653 Kelso and 2,806 Frederick source measurements, unchanged. With the same quantities and plan dependencies, measured-scope finishes remain Kelso **2027-07-23** and Frederick **2025-08-07**.

Verification: TypeScript, unit tests, production build and the refreshed-demo audit; IFC4 validation with IfcOpenShell found **1,225 Kelso / 2,661 Frederick shapes**, with zero schema or geometry errors. The focused unit test also removes one side's grid bubble family and confirms that only that side remains unplaced. No project JSON/PDF was edited.
